STATUTORY AUDITED RESULTS
Audited consolidated financial statements submitted under SEBI LODR Regulations:
Consolidated revenue from operations for the quarter ended December 31, 2025 stood at ₹2,100 crore, reflecting modest revenue growth of 9% year-on-year.
Operating EBITDA was audited and confirmed at ₹390 crore, yielding an operating margin of 18.5%.
The variance against previous management flash figures results from deferral of unbilled software license recognitions subject to milestone verification.
Consolidated debt stood at ₹620 crore, including short-term working capital credit facilities.
