MANAGEMENT PRESS RELEASE: RECORD PERFORMANCE
Apex Technologies Limited, an enterprise cloud software and AI engineering solutions provider, announced stellar preliminary performance for the third quarter ended December 31, 2025:
Consolidated revenue reached ₹2,800 crore, delivering an industry-leading revenue growth of 28% year-on-year.
Operating EBITDA was reported at ₹750 crore, achieving an operating margin of 26.8%.
Management noted exceptional customer additions across North American banking and healthcare clients.
Cash reserves were reported at ₹980 crore with debt reported at ₹150 crore.
Chief Executive Officer commented: "Our proprietary AI platform delivered accelerating contract wins across enterprise customers."
