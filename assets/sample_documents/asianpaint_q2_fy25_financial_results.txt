1. FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS & OPERATING RESULTS
- Consolidated Revenue from Operations for Q2 FY25 was INR 8,028 crore, reflecting a 5.3% YoY decline compared to INR 8,479 crore in Q2 FY24.
- Consolidated Net Profit (PAT) for the quarter fell by 42.4% YoY to INR 695 crore from INR 1,205 crore in Q2 FY24.
- Operating Profit (PBDIT before other income) fell by 27.8% YoY to INR 1,240 crore from INR 1,716 crore in Q2 FY24.
- Consolidated Operating Margin contracted by 480 bps to 15.5% from 20.3% in Q2 FY24, squeezed by raw material price inflation (crude derivatives and titanium dioxide), price reductions implemented in previous quarters, and increased competitive intensity.
- Volume growth in the Domestic Decorative Business was flat (-0.5% YoY), severely impacted by extended monsoons across major consuming states and a delayed festive calendar (Diwali shifting to November).

2. SEGMENT DYNAMICS & INTERNATIONAL OPERATIONS
- Domestic Decorative Paints:
  * Premium and luxury emulsions faced demand deferral; economy interior paints showed relative resilience.
  * Rural demand grew faster than Tier 1/2 urban centers, resulting in adverse product mix.
- Industrial Coatings:
  * Automotive OEM and General Industrial coatings joint ventures (PPG-AP) delivered revenue growth of 6.2% with steady margins.
- Home Décor Business (Kitchens, Bath Fittings, Furnishings, Lighting):
  * Segment revenue stood at INR 245 crore with continued network expansion across Beautiful Homes stores.
- International Business:
  * Revenue was INR 760 crore (down 0.7% YoY in INR terms), impacted by macro currency crises in Egypt and Bangladesh.

3. BALANCE SHEET STRENGTH & WORKING CAPITAL
- Cash and Cash Equivalents plus mutual fund investments stood at INR 3,120 crore.
- Consolidated Net Debt: Negative (Net cash surplus of INR 1,850 crore).
- Gross Debt to Equity was minimal at 0.08x.
- Working capital cycle was maintained at 38 days.

4. DIVIDEND & CORPORATE GOVERNANCE
- The Board declared an Interim Dividend of INR 4.25 per equity share of face value INR 1 each.
- Statutory Auditors (M/s SRBC & CO LLP) issued an unmodified clean review report without qualifications.
- Shareholding: Promoters: 52.63%, FII / FPI: 15.22%, DII: 11.84%, Retail & Public: 20.31%.
