1. FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS & SUMMARY OF RESULTS
- Total Revenue from Operations for Q2 FY25 stood at INR 15,508 crore, reflecting modest 1.9% YoY growth over INR 15,223 crore in Q2 FY24.
- Underlying Volume Growth (UVG) was 3.0% YoY, reflecting gradual demand recovery in rural markets, offset by urban consumption moderation.
- EBITDA stood at INR 3,745 crore, compared to INR 3,797 crore in Q2 FY24 (EBITDA margin: 24.1%, down 80 bps YoY due to tea and palm oil input inflation).
- Profit After Tax (PAT) before exceptional items stood at INR 2,612 crore, down 2.4% YoY compared to INR 2,676 crore in Q2 FY24.
- Net Profit after exceptional items stood at INR 2,612 crore. Exceptional items were nil.

2. SEGMENT PERFORMANCE & CATEGORY DYNAMICS
- Home Care Segment (Fabric Wash, Dishwash, Surface Cleaners):
  * Revenue was INR 5,661 crore (up 7.7% YoY), driven by volume growth across Surf Excel and Rin.
  * Segment operating margin stood at 18.2%.
- Beauty & Wellbeing Segment (Hair Care, Skin Care):
  * Revenue stood at INR 3,421 crore (up 2.5% YoY), led by premium formats in Sunsilk, Tresemme, and Pond's.
  * Segment operating margin was 26.5%.
- Personal Care Segment (Soaps, Oral Care, Deodorants):
  * Revenue declined 4.9% YoY to INR 2,411 crore due to pricing cuts in mass toilet soaps following commodity deflation in earlier quarters.
  * Segment operating margin stood at 22.8%.
- Foods & Refreshment Segment (Tea, Coffee, Ketchup, Ice Creams):
  * Revenue stood at INR 3,803 crore (flat YoY), impacted by double-digit raw tea price inflation.
  * Segment operating margin was 18.0%.

3. STRATEGIC DECISION: SEPARATION OF ICE CREAM BUSINESS
- The Board of Directors on October 23, 2024, approved the separation / demerger of the Ice Cream business (Kwality Wall's, Cornetto, Magnum).
- Ice Cream contributed ~3% of HUL turnover with distinct supply chain and seasonal asset intensity.
- Demerger enables sharper management focus and capital allocation toward high-margin Beauty and Personal Care categories.

4. BALANCE SHEET, CASH FLOW & DIVIDEND
- Total Cash generated from operations in H1 FY25 was INR 5,820 crore.
- Zero debt balance sheet; net cash and bank balances stood at INR 6,850 crore as on September 30, 2024.
- The Board declared a total dividend of INR 29.00 per share (Interim dividend of INR 19.00 and Special dividend of INR 10.00 per share).
- Shareholding: Unilever PLC (Promoter Group): 61.90%, FII / FPI: 12.65%, DII: 13.25%, Public & Retail: 12.20%.
- Statutory Auditors (M/s B S R & Co. LLP) issued an unmodified clean limited review report.
