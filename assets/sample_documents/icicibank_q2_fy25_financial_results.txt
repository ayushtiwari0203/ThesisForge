1. FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS & OPERATING PROFITABILITY
- Standalone Net Profit for Q2 FY25 increased by 14.5% YoY to INR 11,746 crore compared to INR 10,261 crore in Q2 FY24.
- Net Interest Income (NII) increased by 9.5% YoY to INR 20,048 crore in Q2 FY25 from INR 18,308 crore in Q2 FY24.
- Net Interest Margin (NIM) was 4.27% in Q2 FY25 compared to 4.36% in Q1 FY25 and 4.53% in Q2 FY24, reflecting elevated cost of deposits across the banking sector.
- Non-interest income (fee income) grew by 10.8% YoY to INR 6,496 crore, driven by retail banking fees, cards, transaction banking, and trade finance.
- Operating profit before provisions grew by 12.1% YoY to INR 16,043 crore. Cost-to-income ratio stood at 39.4%.

2. ASSET QUALITY & PRUDENTIAL METRICS
- Gross NPA ratio declined to 1.97% as of September 30, 2024, compared to 2.15% on June 30, 2024, and 2.48% on September 30, 2023.
- Net NPA ratio was 0.42% as on September 30, 2024, compared to 0.43% as on June 30, 2024.
- Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR) on non-performing loans was 79.4% as on September 30, 2024.
- Provisions and contingencies for Q2 FY25 were INR 933 crore (0.30% of average advances) compared to INR 583 crore in Q2 FY24.
- Total contingency provisions held outside PCR stood at INR 13,100 crore (1.05% of loans), providing substantial solvency defense.

3. LOAN BOOK COMPOSITION & BALANCE SHEET METRICS
- Domestic loan portfolio grew by 15.7% YoY to INR 12,43,090 crore.
- Retail loan book grew by 12.9% YoY and accounted for 53.6% of the total loan portfolio.
- Business banking portfolio grew by 38.0% YoY, SME loan book grew by 24.3% YoY, and rural portfolio grew by 16.5% YoY.
- Total Period-end Deposits increased by 15.7% YoY to INR 14,97,761 crore.
- Average CASA ratio was 40.0% in Q2 FY25. Average current account deposits grew by 13.0% YoY; average savings account deposits grew by 5.2% YoY.

4. CAPITAL ADEQUACY RATIOS & GOVERNANCE
- Total Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) under RBI Basel III guidelines was 16.63% as on September 30, 2024, with Tier-1 CAR at 15.96% (CET-1 at 15.96%).
- Statutory Auditors (M/s M S K A & Associates and M/s K K Mankeshwar & Co) issued an unmodified (clean) limited review report without adverse qualifications.
- Shareholding pattern: FII / FPI: 44.82%, DII (Mutual Funds & Insurance): 45.45%, Public & Non-Institutions: 9.73%, Promoters: 0.00%.
