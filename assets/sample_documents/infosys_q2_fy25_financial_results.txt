FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE OVERVIEW — Q2 FY 2024-25
Infosys Limited reported consolidated revenue from operations of ₹40,986 crore for Q2 FY25, representing a 5.1% year-on-year growth in INR terms (3.1% year-on-year growth in constant currency terms).
Consolidated Operating Profit (EBIT) for the quarter stood at ₹8,649 crore, resulting in an operating margin of 21.1%.
Net Profit After Tax (PAT) reached ₹6,506 crore, up 4.7% year-on-year compared to ₹6,212 crore in Q2 FY24.
Basic Earnings Per Share (EPS) for the quarter grew by 4.7% YoY to ₹15.71.
The Board of Directors declared an interim dividend of ₹21.00 per equity share of face value ₹5 each.

--- PAGE 2 ---
BUSINESS MOMENTUM & DEAL CONTRACT VALUE
Large deal Total Contract Value (TCV) for Q2 FY25 was robust at $2.4 billion, of which 52% was net new business, underscoring strong enterprise demand for generative AI, cloud migration, and SAP S/4HANA digital core transformations.
Digital revenues contributed 62.4% of total company revenues, continuing to expand as global enterprise clients prioritize automation and generative AI deployments via Infosys Topaz.
Voluntary attrition for IT services on a Last Twelve Months (LTM) basis declined to 12.9%, compared to 14.6% in the previous year, demonstrating stable workforce retention.
Free Cash Flow (FCF) generated during the quarter was exceptionally strong at $840 million (₹7,040 crore), registering a growth of 25.2% year-on-year.

--- PAGE 3 ---
ANNUAL GUIDANCE & STRATEGIC OUTLOOK
Based on robust H1 execution and large deal momentum, management revised upwards its FY 2024-25 constant currency revenue guidance to 3.75% – 4.50% (from the earlier guidance of 3.0% – 4.0%).
Operating margin guidance for the full fiscal year FY 2024-25 was retained at 20.0% – 22.0%.
Total cash and investments as of September 30, 2024 stood at ₹32,150 crore with zero long-term debt, providing superior financial flexibility and fortress balance-sheet security.

--- PAGE 4 ---
RISK FACTORS & MACROECONOMIC HEADWINDS
1. Discretionary Spending Caution: Client decision cycles in financial services and capital markets across North America and Continental Europe remain cautious, leading to delays in uncommitted discretionary proof-of-concept projects.
2. Wage Inflation & Subcontractor Costs: Enterprise salary adjustments and competitive talent acquisition in niche cloud architecture and cybersecurity present continuous operating margin headwinds.
3. Foreign Exchange Volatility: Cross-currency fluctuations across USD, EUR, and GBP affect reported revenue realization, managed via systematic forward derivative hedging.
