1. FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS & SUMMARY OF RESULTS
- Gross Revenue for Q2 FY25 stood at INR 19,328 crore, reflecting an increase of 16.8% YoY over INR 16,550 crore in Q2 FY24.
- Total Segment Revenue (excluding Agri Business leaf tobacco trading spike) grew by 6.8% YoY.
- Profit Before Tax (PBT) stood at INR 6,755 crore, representing a 2.8% YoY increase.
- Profit After Tax (PAT) for the quarter rose by 3.1% YoY to INR 5,078 crore compared to INR 4,927 crore in Q2 FY24.
- Basic EPS for the quarter was INR 4.07 per share.

2. SEGMENTAL PERFORMANCE & OPERATING METRICS
- Cigarettes Segment:
  * Gross revenue stood at INR 8,879 crore (up 7.3% YoY).
  * Segment EBIT increased by 5.1% YoY to INR 5,242 crore.
  * Stability in national tax structures and proactive anti-illicit enforcement supported volume resiliency.
- FMCG Others Segment (Branded Packaged Foods, Personal Care, Stationery, Agarbattis):
  * Revenue reached INR 5,578 crore (up 5.4% YoY), driven by staples (Aashirvaad), biscuits (Sunfeast), snacks (Bingo!), and dairy.
  * Segment EBITDA margin was sustained at 11.0% despite sharp input cost inflation in palm oil, wheat, and cocoa.
- Agri-Business Segment:
  * Revenue grew by 47.0% YoY to INR 3,581 crore, driven by leaf tobacco exports, value-added spices, and coffee trading.
- Paperboards, Paper & Packaging Segment:
  * Revenue was INR 2,114 crore (down 2.1% YoY) due to cheap Chinese carton board imports and depressed domestic prices.
- Hotels Segment:
  * Revenue stood at INR 789 crore (up 12.1% YoY), with Average Room Rates (ARR) and RevPAR reaching record levels.

3. CORPORATE RESTRUCTURING: ITC HOTELS DEMERGER
- National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) Kolkata Bench approved the Scheme of Arrangement for the demerger of ITC Hotels Limited on October 4, 2024.
- Existing ITC shareholders will directly receive 1 equity share of ITC Hotels Limited (face value INR 1) for every 10 shares held in ITC Limited.
- Post-demerger, ITC Limited will retain a 40% strategic stake in ITC Hotels, while 60% will be owned directly by ITC shareholders.

4. BALANCE SHEET, CASH RESERVES & DIVIDENDS
- Cash and Cash Equivalents plus liquid treasury investments exceeded INR 15,800 crore.
- Zero long-term debt; debt-free balance sheet with return on capital employed (ROCE) exceeding 38%.
- Shareholding: Institutional shareholding (FII + DII + LIC + SUUTI): 74.2%, Public & Retail: 25.8%, Promoters: 0.00% (Professionally managed).
- Statutory Auditors (M/s S R B C & CO LLP) issued an unmodified clean limited review report.
