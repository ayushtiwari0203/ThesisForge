1. FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS & REVENUE PERFORMANCE
- Consolidated Revenue from Operations for Q2 FY25 was INR 61,555 crore, registering a robust 20.6% YoY growth over INR 51,024 crore in Q2 FY24.
- International revenues during the quarter stood at INR 32,057 crore, constituting 52% of total consolidated revenue.
- Consolidated Profit After Tax (PAT) stood at INR 3,395 crore, up 5.4% YoY compared to INR 3,223 crore in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.
- Core Engineering, Procurement & Construction (EPC) EBITDA margin stood at 7.7% for the quarter, compared to 8.2% in Q2 FY24, impacted by lingering legacy fixed-price projects and supply-chain escalation.
- Total consolidated EBITDA stood at INR 6,362 crore with an overall operating margin of 10.3%.

2. ORDER BOOK & ORDER INFLOW (OPERATIONAL RUNWAY)
- Order Inflow for Q2 FY25 stood at INR 80,045 crore, representing a 10% YoY increase, led by Infrastructure, Hydrocarbon, Power, and Renewables sectors.
- International orders comprised INR 50,083 crore (62.6% of total quarterly inflows), predominantly from the Middle East (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar).
- The Consolidated Order Book of the Group stood at a record high of INR 5,10,402 crore as on September 30, 2024 (multi-year revenue visibility of 2.7x annual revenue).
- International orders constitute 40% of the total cumulative order book.

3. SEGMENTAL PERFORMANCE
- Infrastructure Projects Segment: Revenue grew 21% YoY to INR 32,230 crore; Order inflow stood at INR 39,215 crore; EBITDA margin was 5.4%.
- Energy Projects Segment (Hydrocarbon & Power): Revenue rose 31% YoY to INR 9,450 crore; EBITDA margin stood at 9.0%.
- Hi-Tech Manufacturing (Heavy Engineering & Defence): Revenue rose 14% to INR 2,120 crore; EBITDA margin was 16.8%.
- IT & Technology Services (LTIMindtree & LTTS): Revenue stood at INR 11,540 crore with EBITDA margin of 17.5%.

4. BALANCE SHEET, CASH FLOW & CAPITAL ALLOCATION
- Gross Debt stood at INR 1,18,400 crore; Net Debt to Equity ratio improved to 0.62x compared to 0.70x in September 2023.
- Net Working Capital to Revenue ratio was managed at 12.2% compared to 16.7% in September 2023, reflecting rigorous receivables management.
- Credit Rating: CRISIL AAA / Stable, ICRA AAA / Stable (highest credit standing).
- Statutory Auditors (M/s Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP) issued an unmodified review report without any audit qualifications.
- Shareholding: FII: 23.40%, DII: 38.65%, Public & Trust: 37.95%, Promoters: 0.00% (Professionally managed enterprise).
