UNAUDITED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE QUARTER AND HALF YEAR ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2025 (Q2 FY26)

--- PAGE 1 ---
FINANCIAL RESULTS SUMMARY — Q2 FY 2025-26
Solaris Power Tech Limited reported solid quarterly performance for Q2 FY26:
Consolidated revenue from operations for the quarter stood at ₹1,340 crore compared to ₹1,160 crore in Q2 FY25, representing a 15.5% year-on-year increase.
Consolidated EBITDA for Q2 FY26 rose to ₹315 crore with an operating margin of 23.5%.
Profit After Tax (PAT) for the quarter came in at ₹178 crore, reflecting stable execution across renewable grid projects in Gujarat and Rajasthan.
New order inflows during the quarter totaled ₹2,100 crore, taking the active order backlog to ₹12,650 crore.

--- PAGE 2 ---
MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY & CAPITAL STRUCTURE
Consolidated debt as of September 30, 2025 stood at ₹2,080 crore, indicating debt reduction of ₹70 crore from March 2025 levels.
Cash and liquid mutual fund balances stood at ₹510 crore, ensuring comfortable liquidity buffers.
Finance costs for the quarter were ₹54 crore compared to ₹51 crore in the corresponding prior quarter.
Management reaffirmed full-year FY26 guidance of 18% consolidated revenue growth and operating margins within the 22.5% to 24.0% range.
There are no pending material litigations or defaults on banking loan covenants.
