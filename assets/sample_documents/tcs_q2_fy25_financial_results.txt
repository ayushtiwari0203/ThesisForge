FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS — Q2 FY 2024-25
Tata Consultancy Services Limited reported consolidated revenue of ₹64,259 crore ($7.67 billion) for the quarter ended September 30, 2024, an increase of 7.6% YoY in INR terms and 5.5% YoY in constant currency.
Operating Profit (EBIT) for Q2 FY25 stood at ₹15,465 crore ($1.85 billion), representing an operating margin of 24.1%, expanding by 20 basis points YoY despite annual wage hike cycles.
Net Profit After Tax (PAT) reached ₹11,909 crore ($1.42 billion), growing 5.0% year-on-year.
Basic Earnings Per Share (EPS) for the quarter stood at ₹32.74.
The Board of Directors declared a second interim dividend of ₹10.00 per equity share of face value ₹1 each.

ORDER BOOK, DEAL WINS & INDUSTRY VERTICALS
1. Order Book Momentum:
   - Total Contract Value (TCV) signed in Q2 FY25 reached $8.6 billion across 14 mega deals and multiple enterprise AI transformations.
   - BFSI TCV was $2.9 billion; Consumer Business TCV stood at $1.2 billion.
2. Growth Drivers by Vertical:
   - Energy, Resources & Utilities grew 7.0% YoY in CC.
   - Manufacturing grew 5.3% YoY in CC.
   - Regional Markets (India) surged 95.2% YoY driven by the execution of the state-owned BSNL nationwide 4G/5G deployment contract.
   - BFSI vertical contracted marginally by 0.1% YoY, indicating lingering hesitation in discretionary US mortgage and investment banking IT budgets.
3. AI and Generative AI Engagements:
   - Enterprise AI pipeline doubled over six months to $1.5 billion, with over 600 active AI engagements utilizing TCS AI WisdomNext suite.

--- PAGE 3 ---
HUMAN CAPITAL, ATTRITION & CASH FLOWS
1. Workforce Metrics:
   - Total headcount as of September 30, 2024 was 612,724 employees, adding 5,726 net employees in Q2 FY25.
   - IT services LTM attrition further moderated to 12.3%, well within the targeted management band of 11%–13%.
2. Cash Conversion and Financial Position:
   - Net cash provided by operating activities was ₹11,932 crore, representing 100.2% of net income.
   - Free cash flow for the quarter stood at ₹10,950 crore.
   - Total cash, bank balances, and liquid mutual fund investments stood at ₹46,280 crore with zero debt on the balance sheet.
   - Return on Equity (ROE) annualized stood at 48.7% and Return on Invested Capital (ROIC) was 58.1%.

RISK FACTORS & STRATEGIC UNCERTAINTIES
1. BFSI Sub-sector Sluggishness: While long-term transformational demand remains intact, North American banking institutions continue to delay non-critical discretionary spend pending interest rate cuts and economic clarity.
2. Margin Pressure from BSNL Rollout: The large-scale hardware and network integration portion of the India regional contract carries lower gross margins than traditional software export services, requiring efficiency levers in offshore delivery to maintain target 26-28% margin corridors.
3. Geopolitical and Visa Restrictions: Legislative proposals concerning H-1B visa fees and localized hiring mandates in North America and Western Europe necessitate continued local talent recruitment and training investments.
