ANNUAL REPORT & FINANCIAL DISCLOSURES — FY 2024-25

--- PAGE 1 ---
OPERATING HIGHLIGHTS & FINANCIAL SUMMARY
Zenith Infrastructure Limited operates engineering, procurement and toll highway infrastructure assets across Northern and Central India.
During FY 2024-25, consolidated revenue from construction contracts was ₹3,420 crore against ₹3,180 crore in the prior fiscal year.
Consolidated EBITDA was reported at ₹580 crore, with reported EBITDA margin of 17.0%.
Reported Net Profit After Tax stood at ₹112 crore.
Order book as of March 31, 2025 stood at ₹7,850 crore across regional highway corridor concessions.

--- PAGE 2 ---
BALANCE SHEET & DEBT COVENANT STATUS
Total consolidated debt as of March 31, 2025 stood at ₹5,800 crore against tangible net worth of ₹1,700 crore, resulting in a debt-to-equity ratio of 3.41x.
The Company's debt-to-equity ratio of 3.41x breaches the maximum leverage covenant threshold of 2.00x stipulated by primary consortium lenders.
Discussions are currently ongoing with debt syndication lead banks for debt restructuring and tenor extension.
Finance costs for FY 2024-25 consumed ₹510 crore, exhausting 88% of total operating EBITDA.

--- PAGE 3 ---
REGULATORY SCRUTINY & PENDING LEGAL INVESTIGATION
1. SEBI Show-Cause Notice: The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) issued a comprehensive show-cause notice on January 14, 2025 regarding alleged failure to disclose related party sub-contracting transactions valued at ₹420 crore.
2. Regulatory Action & Penalties: Enforcement directorate inquiries and regulatory scrutiny are pending regarding subcontracting advances paid to offshore promoter-linked entities.
3. Customer Concentration Risk: Top 2 state transport concessions account for 78% of total outstanding receivables and unbilled work-in-progress, resulting in severe liquidity vulnerability.

--- PAGE 4 ---
INDEPENDENT AUDITOR'S REPORT & ADVERSE OBSERVATIONS
To the Shareholders of Zenith Infrastructure Limited:
We have audited the consolidated financial statements of Zenith Infrastructure Limited.
Basis for Qualified Opinion:
We draw attention to Note 34 concerning the recoverability of unbilled advances amounting to ₹420 crore. 
In our opinion, there is a material weakness in internal financial controls over related party reporting.
The accompanying financial statements reflect substantial uncertainty regarding the Company's ability to continue as a going concern in the event loan acceleration clauses are enforced by lenders.
